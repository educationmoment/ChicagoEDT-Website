This subsystem encompasses the backbone of the drone's electrical and control infrastructure, integration of all onboard and ground station hardware, and power systems onboard the drone.

Custom power converter board

This board converts power from the control systems battery to multiple voltages needed for powering onboard electronic components, outputting 5V for the payload carrier, 12V for the companion computer, and 24V for the wireless access point. It can be powered between 13.4-21V DC, allowing a 4-5S lithium-ion battery to be used as a power source.

Initially, we explored commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) solutions for power conversion, however, could not find hardware that outputs our required voltages in a compact footprint, especially with requiring a 24V step up cconverter. Therefore, we designed 5V and 12V buck converters and a 24V boost converter on a single board with a footprint of 45mm x 45mm, a size that can easily fit onboard a drone, while only using components with 0603 or larger packages to facilitate manual board assembly.

Additional output ports are added to accomodate for the possibility of additional hardware such as a winch, FPV video transmitter, active cooling, lighting, etc. as well as potential use in other EDT projects.
