How to Donate

Thank you for considering a donation to the Chicago Student Engineering Design Team! Below are instructions for donations online or by check.

Online Donations

  1. Visit: https://payment.give.uic.edu/campaigns/48413/donations/new
  2. Fill out the giving form. In the Designation dialog box, select the option "other" and then type UIC Engineering Design Team Fund
  3. Select the payment method and complete the transation.

For additional giving instructions or help, you can visit https://advance.uic.edu/ways-to-give-info/

Donations Via Check

  1. Make a check payable to University of Illinois Foundation.
  2. In the Memo section, make sure to include: "UIC Engineering Design Fund".
  3. Mail your donation check to: University of Illinois Foundation, P.O. Box 734500, Chicago, IL, 60673-4500
  4. Receive recognition on our website and in team materials

For additional giving instructions or help, you can visit https://advance.uic.edu/ways-to-give-info/

If you or your company would like to be featured in our sponsored section on this website for your donation(s), contact use at: chicagoedtcontact@gmail.com.
