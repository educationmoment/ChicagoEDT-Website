Mission to the Moon

What is Lunabotics?

The Lunabotics team is a student-led engineering design team within EDT that competes in NASA's Lunabotics Challenge. The competition is held annually at Kennedy Space Center, where universities across the country design and build a rover with the goal of autonomously excavating lunar regolith using NASA's Systems Engineering principles to manage the project.

Team Goals

  • Design and build a rover from scratch.
  • Learn to apply NASA's Systems Engineering principles efficiently.
  • Compete and do well in the annual Lunabotics Challenge at Kennedy Space Center.
  • Have fun and get to know one another!
